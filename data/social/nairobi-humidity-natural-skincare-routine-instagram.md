Nairobi air is 72% humidity. Your seven-step routine was designed for a city half as humid.

Most skincare content was written for Western climates where the air runs dry. In Nairobi, the atmosphere is already doing part of the moisturising work.

The problem is not the humidity. It is that nothing is holding the moisture in place. Your skin pulls water in from the air, but without the right products in the right order, it escapes just as fast.

Three steps handle this.

First: aloe vera on damp skin right after washing. It draws water toward the skin surface and holds it there. Takes 60 seconds to absorb.

Second: shea butter on top, pea-sized, warmed in your palms first. It seals what the aloe drew in.

Third: sunscreen in the morning, a generous shea application at night. Unrefined shea contains compounds that support your skin's overnight repair work.

Four products. Three steps. Built for the climate you actually live in.
