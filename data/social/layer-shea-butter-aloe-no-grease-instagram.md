Your shea butter is not greasy. You are applying it at the wrong time.

Shea butter is an occlusive. It seals moisture in, it does not add moisture. Aloe vera is a humectant. It draws water into your skin's outer layer. These two do different jobs, and they work best when you use them in the right order.

Most people apply shea butter first, or apply it to completely dry skin. When there is no water to seal in, the slow-absorbing oleic acid sits on the surface. That is the grease you are feeling.

The fix is simple: aloe vera on damp skin first, then shea butter on top within 60 seconds. One pea-sized amount for your full face. Press it in, do not rub.

The result is not just less grease. Your skin holds moisture differently across the day.
